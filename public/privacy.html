Privacy

What this page collects.

A random id in a cookie named pk_v, kept 90 days, so one visitor is not counted twice. Event rows: the page viewed, which buttons were tapped (Reserve, Yearly, Monthly), whether the plan price came into view, seconds on the page, the utm tags in the link, the website the link was opened from, the device type, the browser's identification string, and whether the page opened inside the TikTok, Instagram or Facebook app. An email address and the plan picked, only if an email is typed in.

Why.

To count how many visitors from our videos pick a plan, to check the page works inside the apps, and to send one email when Parakeet launches.

What is not collected.

No name, no location, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics, no pixels. Nothing here is sold or shared with anyone: never shared, never sold.

How long.

Event rows are kept 90 days, then deleted. Email addresses are kept until launch plus 90 days, then deleted. They are used for one email, on launch day, and nothing else.

Where.

The page and its counter run on Netlify; the data is stored there, in the United States.

In the app.

The app counts what people do in it, so we can see what works and fix what doesn’t: setup’s pages, a week built, the list opened, a price typed, a dinner marked cooked, a thumb, a swap, the paywall and the free week. Each count carries a random id the app makes when it’s installed. Some also carry the store chain, the first three digits of the ZIP code, the household’s size, the diet, the budget, the week’s estimated total and which dinners were picked. It never sends a name, an email, your exact location or full ZIP code, the phone’s identifiers, your notes, your own list items or the prices you type. Your notes, your lists and the prices you type stay on your phone. The counts are stored on Netlify, in the United States, and kept 2 years, then deleted.

Deletion.

Any address or id is deleted on request, at any time: support.parakeet@gmail.com.
